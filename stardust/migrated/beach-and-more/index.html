Activities for everyone

Culture is great, and so is to be in contact with nature. However, we can not forget where Casa Argentera is located: close enough to many places that worth a visit! Just have a look!

The Mediterranean secrets

Long sandy beaches awaits you at the Costa Daurada. Indeed the coast around Cambrils got its name buy the glittering stone dust in the water, taken out in the seas from the rivers. Nice small calas and hidden coves are waiting for your discovery. Go for a stroll on the harbour to see what the fishers caught and try the excellent fish tapas in Cambrils. Or go for a tour in Catamaran to see the coast, or even try to swim with hundreds of bluefin tuna.

Total relax in Aquatonic

Only 12 km from Casa Argentera, Aquatonic is immersed in a magnificent botanical garden of 4 hectares with centuries-old species. Its waters are classified as hyperthermal, rich in ions, bicarbonate, sulfate, chlorine, sodium, calcium, magnesium, lithium and potassium. enjoy multiple thermo-leisure games in a surface area of more than 1,000 square meters in hot springs.

Port Aventura World

Port Aventura World needs no introduction: a huge amusement parc for all the family, great fun and amazing shows.
