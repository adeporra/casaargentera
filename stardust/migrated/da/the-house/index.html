Enjoy your stay in this historical building, carefully refurbished maintaining its original 1920’s charm, while ensuring a comfortable stay.

Casa Argentera can be rented as a whole or by individual apartments. Each apartment is on its own floor and has its own fireplace. Each floor has been tastefully decorated with rural appeal: you can try your hand at cooking in a masonry stove on the groundfloor, watch life go by from the stone carved balcony on the first floor, or read a book on a private terrace.

The whole house

For groups up to 15 people. Enjoy each other’s company without compromising comfort

The garden

Enjoy the Mediterranean weather sunbathing, or cooling off in the swimming pool, or barbecuing, or resting in the chill out sofa.

The Penthouse

The second floor appartment with beautiful curved windows that let in plenty of light. The views of the old bell tower of the village is also remarkable. For 4 people.

The noble Floor

The first floor appartment with many original details, such as the tiled floors, each room with its own pattern. For 5-6 people.

The ground floor

It is the ground floor appartment, where you can try cooking in the masonry stove. It is rented with the rest of the house.
