HERE YOUR STORY BEGINS

Casa Argentera, a historical village situated in a small catalan mountain village, between the famous wine region Priorat and the Mediterranean Sea

A place where time stands still, and unique traditions stays alive. Situated in a valley, in a tiny authentic Catalan village with cobblestone streets lined with ever blooming flowers and a majestic view of the castle of Escornalbou. A place where your story begins.
