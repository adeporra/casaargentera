Groups, big families… Enjoy each other’s company without compromising comfort

Fully refurbished and adapted for bigger families, or small groups. The ground floor is equipped with a dining room that fits 15 people, and a garden with exterior dining space for all. Each floor has its own kitchen and dining room, and various spaces for retreating, such as terraces, balconies, reading corners and sofa chill out places. Plenty of space for being yourself, and being together.
