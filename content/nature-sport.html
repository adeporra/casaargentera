Nature makes you feel better. There is plenty of it here

The house is located in a tiny village of 130 inhabitants, in the valley of the Catalan coastal mountain range L’Argentera. Just one step and you are in nature. The area is perfect for  mountain or road biking, hiking or horseback riding. It is up to you!

For cycling enthusiasts

Whether you are to thrilling MTB paths, or road cycling, we have more than 15 carefully designed routes running almost entirely along secondary roads that provide a pleasant mix of natural and rural scenary, magnificent climbs and winding descents, varying from 18 to 182 km with different heights and difficulty.
You will find more than 2,000 km of quiet roads with excellent quality surfaces. Contact us to get the routes and schedule of the next Gran Fondo.

Walking, Trekking or Hiking the route is marked

Hiking in this area is something you should not miss. Explore the mountains, well connected with many hiking routes at different difficulty levels, like the GR7, but also to coves, and natural stone arches formed by nature. Again, we can help by showing you the best routes to suit your needs.

Horse lovers

Enjoy quiet horseback riding through small roads and paths surrounded by olive and hazelnut fields, through dried river banks and the endless vegetable fields in the Baix Camp, where the mountains fade out and you join the Mediterreanean Sea.
