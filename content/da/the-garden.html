Enjoy the Mediterranean weather to it’s fullest

You have direct acces to the garden from all floors. The garden has various areas to help you enjoy the experience you are looking for. It is divided into two zones. One zone has the swimming pool and a wooden sundeck with relaxing lounge chairs. The other one has built in BBQ and Paella equipment. a place for dining and a chillout corner. There is also a swing in the linden tree, and a slide for the children, or childish, to play.
