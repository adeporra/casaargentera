We are a Danish-Catalan couple with 3 children, who have lived and worked in L’Argentera for more than 10 years

We enjoy building projects, travelling and visiting new places. By bike, horse, kayak, or walking, there are always new places to be discovered. So whether you would like some advice, or you would rather find your own way in our lovely territory, we are here to help with anything you need. We want you to enjoy and make the most of your stay.
