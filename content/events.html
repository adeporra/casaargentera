Celebrate your event in Casa Argentera

We will be happy to help you with the organization of the event you have in mind.

We have a big dinning/meeting room to small parties or meetings, team buildings, or workshops or courses. If the weather is good (as usual), the garden is also a perfect option.

Here are some suggestions, to be combined in any way that suits you:

  • tapas-workshop,
  • wine- or olive oil tastings
  • spent a day with a shepherd
  • meditation or yoga
  • painting or paella contests
  • calçotadas
  • bike training camps

Maybe you would like your course or training to end with a competition in a local race, e.g. Gran Fondo Priorat or Cambrils, or participate in the annual quick painting contest, or for your team-building event to include a paella contest, or a tapas workshops, just write us with your ideas and we will help you organize.
