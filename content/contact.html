Some directions to get to Casa Argentera

Contact us for any further information or ideas

plane

By plane

Reus Airport is only 35 minutes away by car, and you can check Ryanair for flights. Barcelona Airport is 1 hour 35 minutes away by car.
From both airports you can either rent a car or take the train or bus to get to Argentera

car-alt

By car

- From Barcelona,
take either the motorway AP7 or A7 southbound and get off in Cambrils (exit 37) south of Salou. Then take the T-312 direction Montbrio del Camp, here you follow signs to Riudecanyes, Duesaïgues and L'Argentera. It's about 15 kms drive.
- From Reus,
take T-310 direction Riudoms, and you don't leave the road until you arrive to Montbrió del Camp, here you follow as described before.
- Falset,
take N-420 direction Reus and halfway (by the mountain Coll de la Teixeta you take T-313 direction Duesaïgues and follow signs to L'Argentera.

train

By train

There are 1,1 km from the station to the house.
There are 3 daily arrivals from Barcelona to the station L'Argentera-Duesaïgues.

bus

By bus

There are 2 arrivals and departures on Monday and Thursday from Reus. Bus leaving Reus 8.45 and 15.15 and bus leaving L'Argentera 9.30 and 16.00. The busstop in L'Argentera is in front of the house, and in Reus at Plaza de la Oca.
